## In
- Country: **US only**
- Period: 2019 to 2026, quarterly (COVID quarters handled explicitly, see [[Measurement Choices (US)]])
- Shock: ChatGPT launch, Nov 2022 (post from 2023Q1, base 2022Q4)
- Outcomes: employment (main); under-30 share; value added per worker (extension)
- Exposure: Anthropic observed exposure, Microsoft AI applicability, Felten AIOE; Eloundou as robustness
- Splits: age (headline), sex, seniority proxy, education (to decide)

- Long-run general equilibrium effects
