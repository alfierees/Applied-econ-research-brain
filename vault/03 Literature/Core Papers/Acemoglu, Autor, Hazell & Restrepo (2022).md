# Acemoglu, Autor, Hazell & Restrepo (2022): Artificial Intelligence and Jobs: Evidence from Online Vacancies

| | |
|---|---|
| **Question** | Does AI adoption reduce hiring at establishments whose tasks AI can perform? |
| **Data** | US near-universal online vacancies (Burning Glass), 2010-2018, establishment level |
| **Method** | Regressions of AI and non-AI hiring on occupational AI exposure, exposed vs unexposed establishments over time |
| **Main finding** | Exposed establishments post more AI vacancies and cut non-AI hiring; aggregate occupation/industry effects too small to detect |
| **How we use it** | Exposure × time design on vacancies; pre-generative-AI benchmark for the 'small aggregate effect' debate |
