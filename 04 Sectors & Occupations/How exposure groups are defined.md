# How exposure groups are defined

- **Main:** continuous exposure score per occupation (standardised).
- **Robustness:** top quartile vs zero exposure (Anthropic's split), so US results compare directly.
- **Industry exposure (Design B):** employment-weighted average of its occupations' scores, using the 2019 occupation mix (US: OEWS staffing patterns; Israel: LFS industry × occupation).
- Sector notes in `Exposed/` and `Low Exposure (Controls)/` are a reading aid. The regression uses occupation scores, not these labels.
