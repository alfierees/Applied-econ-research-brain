# Design B: Industry productivity (extension)

ln(VA/worker)_jt = θ (Exposure_j × Post_t) + α_j + δ_t + ε_jt

- Industry exposure = employment-weighted average of occupation scores, 2019 mix.
- US: BEA quarterly (~70 industries); BLS annual as check. Israel dropped.
- Whether to keep Design B at all: ask Dr. Hadass.
- **θ in words:** % change in output per worker in more exposed industries, after vs before.
- Report ln VA and ln employment separately too: falling employment alone raises VA/worker.
- Caveat: productivity J-curve ([[Brynjolfsson, Rock & Syverson (2021)]]).
